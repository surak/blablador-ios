Blablador,
native on your devices

Chat with the open models of Helmholtz Blablador on Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android. Talk to it, search the web, send photos and PDFs, create images and give it a voice, even your own. It remembers what you tell it and learns the way you like things done.

Download for Mac Download for Android View source
Mac: Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 14 or later · Android 8.0 or later

See it in action

  • Ask about today. Blablador searches the web, reads the pages and answers with numbered sources. Ask about a person or place and it shows pictures too.
  • Just chat. Blablador remembers what matters, about you and the things you discuss, in every later chat, and you can see and edit what it remembers in Settings.
  • Teach it a task, like how you want meeting notes. It offers to save the steps as a skill and follows them next time.
  • Tap + for web search, image creation, speech, photos, the camera or a PDF.
  • Send a photo and ask what it shows.
  • Turn on Speak and type what should be said. Add pauses, laughs or whispers from the Expressions menu.
  • Build a knowledge base from your PDFs, switch it on with the books button or by typing # and its name, and ask questions across all of them.
  • Attach a PDF and ask questions about it. Scanned pages are read with OCR right on your device.

Everything Blablador can do

💬

All Blablador models

Streaming replies from every chat model on the server. DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash by default, switch any time.

💡

Long-term memory

Blablador learns from every chat: facts about you, your projects, decisions and conclusions, and uses them in later chats. Related memories are linked, so asking about one person or project brings back the facts connected to it. It also finds earlier conversations when you refer to them. See, edit or delete every memory in Settings.

🪄

Skills

Write instructions once for the tasks you repeat, such as meeting notes or press releases, and Blablador follows them whenever the task comes up. It can suggest new skills for you to save. Share and import skills as Markdown files.

🐍

Runs Python for you

Ask for a calculation, an analysis or a plot and Blablador writes and runs Python right on your device, with numpy, pandas and matplotlib. Attach a CSV, Excel or JSON file and ask about your own data; plots appear in the reply.

🔌

Connect your tools

Add MCP servers, such as GitLab, a calendar or your own services, and Blablador can use their tools in every chat, each call with your OK.

📤

From anywhere

Share a web page, photo, PDF or spreadsheet to Blablador from any app, open files with it, or use the Shortcuts actions: ask and get the answer, run a skill, add a memory.

∑

Maths, diagrams, previews

Formulas are typeset, Mermaid diagrams drawn and HTML or SVG shown live. Edit any message and switch between versions, or regenerate a reply with another model.

🎬

Videos with sound

Describe a scene and its sounds, and MiniMax-H3 makes a short clip in landscape, portrait or square. Continue a clip from its last frame, or bring a photo to life.

🧠

Watch it think

Reasoning models show their thinking as it happens, folded away once the answer starts. Slow, deep-thinking models get as long as you allow in Settings.

🎙️

Voice in, voice out

Tap the microphone to dictate with on-device speech recognition. Tap the speaker button under any reply to hear it read aloud.

🌐

Web search

The model searches the web through Blablador's own search engine when it needs current information, and cites its sources.

📷

Photos and camera

Attach pictures from your photos or files, take a photo, paste a copied image, or drag images and PDFs onto the chat on the Mac. Then ask about them with vision-capable models.

🗣️

Expressive speech, cloned voices

Have text spoken with Drama 3, with feelings, pauses, laughs and whispers. Clone a voice from a short recording, an audio or video file, or a link to one. Copy or share a spoken reply as an audio file, for example into Messages.

🧑‍💻

Agent mode on the Mac

Point Blablador at a folder and let it work like a coding agent: it reads and edits files, runs builds and tests, and fixes what fails. Every shell command waits for your OK.

🔀

Decisions with probabilities

Ask the Clef decision model typed questions: yes or no, which option, what score. Write and save your own, or start from 73 examples for routing, triage, business and science, and see the probability of every answer.

📚

Knowledge bases

Keep collections of PDFs and notes on your device and chat with them. Blablador finds the right passages and cites them, so you can check every answer.

📄

PDFs, even scanned

Attach PDFs and ask about them. Text is extracted on your device. Scanned pages and photos of documents are read with OCR, on your device or with Blablador's LightOnOCR, which keeps tables intact. Works with every model.

🎨

Create and edit images

Generate images with FLUX.2-dev or Qwen-Image 2.1, and edit any image with Qwen-Image: tap Edit on it and describe the change, or just ask.

🔄

Always up to date

The Mac app updates itself: when a new version is out, click Update and Blablador restarts with it. On Android, a banner links you to the new version.

✅

Plans and follows through

For bigger tasks Blablador writes a plan, ticks off each step as it works and keeps going until it's done. After a task like that it offers to save what it did as a skill for next time.

⏰

Works while you're away

Replies keep going when you switch chats or leave the app, and a notification tells you when they're ready. Schedule prompts, like a morning news summary, to run by themselves.

📅

Your calendar and contacts

Turn it on and ask “What's on tomorrow?”, “Add lunch with Maria on Friday” or “What's Tom's number?”. Nothing is added without your OK.

✋

You decide what it may do

Set each kind of tool to Allow, Ask or Never. Every reply shows which tools it used, how long it took and how many tokens, and you can run a step again.

@

Everything one tap away

The + button shows attachments, tools, knowledge and connected services at a glance. Type @ to use a tool, skill or knowledge base for just one message.

🗂️

Your data, encrypted

Chats, memories, skills, images, recordings and knowledge bases stay on your device, encrypted with a key kept in the Keychain (Android Keystore on Android), like your API key. Text from web pages and documents can't rewrite Blablador's memory.

Install on your Mac

  1. Download the .dmg from the latest release, open it and drag Blablador into Applications.
  2. This build is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it at first. Open Terminal and run: 
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Blablador.app
  3. Open Blablador and paste your Blablador API key in Settings. Don't have one? Create one on Helmholtz Codebase with the read_user scope.
  4. That's it for installing: from version 2.2 on, Blablador offers new versions itself and installs them with one click.
On macOS 15 Sequoia and later, right-click → Open no longer bypasses the warning. Use the command above, or try to open the app once and then click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Install on Android

  1. On your phone, download the .apk from the latest release.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps, then tap Install. The app is signed by the developer, not distributed through Google Play.
  3. Open Blablador and paste your Blablador API key in Settings. Don't have one? Create one on Helmholtz Codebase with the read_user scope.
  4. When a new version is out, a banner above the chat links to it. Download and open the new APK the same way; your chats and settings stay.
